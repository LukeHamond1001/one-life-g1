# Pre-registration of the watch (W1): learning to act from a recording without its acts — DRAFT until its birth commit names it

Written 2026-10-21 14:55, with P4 running (docs/archive/PREREGISTRATION_P4.md). Committed in its final form before the copies it governs
are born; nothing in sections 1 to 4 is edited after; sections 5 and 6 are appended, dated.

## 1. The claim, honestly framed

The frontier's pipeline is bottlenecked on action-labelled data. This body has inverse models (act_inv: each motor effector's act read
from its body sense's change), earned on its own acts, and its act lesson already takes their reading as a label at positions where
the body moved without its own act, weighted by their reliability. W1 (commit: the watch) extends that rule to a whole recording:
from a tick on, the recording's act columns are not read; each act is the inverse model's reading of the senses' change to the next
frame, forced as a guided act and taught at the model's earned weight; the inverse models learn nothing while watching.

What this is: **the robot's own first-person senses with the acts hidden** — action-free sensor logs (teleop without command logging,
another robot's streams), the same body, its own eyes. A recipe with a precedent (VPT: an inverse model from a small labelled set
labelling the rest), here inside one brain that also learns its forecasts, values and nights from the same recording. What this is
not: third-person or human video, which needs correspondence across bodies; a different road.

## 2. The copies

Two fresh brains, the same seed (4) and the same diet (the recordings on disk at the birth, tasks8 from s8140 and tasks9, in the
relive's order; the generator slots not read: a fixed diet for a paired comparison), P4's body
(`act_pred_rate=8.0 own_words=1 own_words_split=1 eye_dino=1 orient_earned=1 place_channels=0 efference_stream=0 face_form=foresee`):
- **labelled**: the relive as P4's students have it, to night 3 and 2,000 ticks on (74,000 ticks).
- **watched**: the same with `--watch-from 24000`: the first day labelled (the inverse models' warm-up: act_inv's kappa in P4's students
  at 14,000 ticks was gaze 0.62, walk 0.66, hands 0.76 to 0.83, the arms 0.29 to 0.53), then watched through nights 1 to 3.

## 3. The measurements (as P3 section 3, at 74,000 ticks)

The own-state ruler (1,500 ticks on seed 7002, act_pred's guess on the expert's side for the gaze's yaw and pitch and the walk's go);
the home test (6,000 ticks, seed 51; the body families); the held-out ruler. The watched copy's inverse reliabilities at the switch
and the share of its watched positions read as the rest are recorded.

## 4. The rule, written before any number

The watch is shown to learn acting where the watched copy's own-state gaze yaw and pitch same-side shares are each at least 0.7 of
the labelled copy's, and its home-test body rate at least 0.7 of the labelled copy's (pooled over the four families). Below that on
any of the three it is a negative result, written as one, with the inverse models' reliabilities beside it as the first suspect.
No copy is added, no switch changed, between the birth and the reading.
