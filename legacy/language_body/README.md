# The language body (2026-09-04 to 2026-09-24)

Before the robot, the same architecture lived as a language body: a stream of characters, a served page a person typed to, the
person's face as its reward (two keys, a smile and a frown), and the same nights. It was raised for three weeks by a typist program
and a person. On 2026-09-24 the architecture moved into the simulated Unitree G1 (`docs/SIM_DESIGN.md`), and the language body's
code (`body/serve.py`, `body/teacher.py`, `body/caregiver.py`, `body/fastlife.py`) stayed in the tree as the lineage it is.

What is here, as it was:

- `ARCHITECTURE.md` the organs as they stood before the sim · `BODY_SPEC.md` the specification, with every defect found and its test ·
  `ITERATIONS.md` the ledger, items 1 to 52 · `DIARY_BODY.md` the diary of the raising · `CURRICULUM.md` the earlier staged curriculum.
- `ops/` what ran the served body day and night (its flags in `ops/BASE_FLAGS.txt`, still read by `tools/determinism_check.py`'s
  language profile, whose pinned digest the project keeps), its reloads, the teacher's brief and the review of 2026-09-22.
- `tools/` the language body's instruments: its rulers, its copies, its store read back, and `archive/` of the earlier probes.

Paths inside these files are the machine's at the time (`/Users/.../project`); they are kept as records, not maintained as programs.
