# The simulated world before the G1 (2026-09-22 to 24)

The first simulated child was a custom body in a high chair (`make_highchair_onearm.py`, `highchair_onearm.xml`; `sim_look.py` is the
first look at it), then the same child in a living room with a drawn parent (`make_livingroom_customchild.py`, `livingroom_customchild.xml`,
`parent_face_customchild.py`, `scene_customchild.py`). On 2026-09-24 the child became the stock Unitree G1 (`body/sim/make_g1room.py`,
`g1scene.py`), which took the room's design and her face from these. Kept as they were; nothing imports them (moved here 2026-10-21).
